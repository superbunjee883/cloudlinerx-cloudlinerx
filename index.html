What's the catch with a free sample?

No catch. A sample takes days and shows you exactly what you'd get — that's a stronger pitch than any slide deck. If you don't like it, you keep the link and owe nothing.

How do you charge?

Custom quote on your site and needs: one build price, one monthly care price. Tell me what you're paying now or what you need and I'll tailor both. No fixed menus, no surprise fees.

Do I own my site?

Yes. Domain in your name, files yours to keep. The care plan covers hosting and upkeep while we're working together — leave any time and take everything with you.

I already have someone / a nephew / Wix. Why switch?

Maybe don't. If your current setup brings calls, keep it. Send me the link and I'll tell you honestly whether a rebuild would pay for itself — including when the answer is no.

What counts as a monthly edit?

Text updates, phone and hours changes, adding a service, swapping photos, banner updates, fixing a broken link. Full rebuilds or new pages are separate projects, quoted upfront.
