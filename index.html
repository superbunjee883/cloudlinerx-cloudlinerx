Where the tea comes from

Checked before it's poured.

The team inspects farms on site, and the teas are tested before they reach the shelf.
  • Pure teas go through 72-item testing by Intertek, covering pesticide residues and heavy metals.
  • Floral teas are tested for 12 pesticide residue items.
  • Farm inspections and interviews happen on site.
